# Lead Activities Management

Lead activities help users maintain complete interaction history with leads and improve follow-up management throughout the sales lifecycle.

The Activities section allows CRM users to:

- Create and manage notes
- Create follow-up tasks
- Schedule meetings
- Track lead updates
- Maintain communication history
- Collaborate through comments

All activities are maintained inside the lead timeline for better visibility and tracking.

---

## Activities Panel Overview

The Lead Detail page contains an **Activities Panel** with multiple tabs such as:

- Activities
- Notes
- Tasks
- Meetings
- Emails

These tabs help users organize and manage lead interactions efficiently.

---
## Creating and Managing Notes

The **Notes** feature allows users to create and manage lead-related notes within the Activities section.

Notes help users track discussions, customer requirements, follow-ups, and important updates related to a lead.

---

### Step 1: Open the Lead Record

- Navigate to the **Leads** Module.
- Open the required lead record.
- Go to the **Notes** tab.

---

### Step 2: Click on Add

- Click the **Add** button.

This opens the **Note Editor** window.

![Create Note](/img/Leads/CreateNote.png)

---

### Note Editor Features

The Note Editor allows users to:

- Enter note content
- Format text
- Insert links
- Add images
- Create bullet lists
- Add tables
- Add follow-up tasks

---

### Step 3: Enter Note Content

Enter the required note details in the editor.

#### Example

- Customer requested product demo.
- Follow-up discussion completed.
- Pricing shared with customer.

---

### Step 4: Submit the Note

After entering the details:

- Click the **Submit** button.

The note will be saved and displayed under the **Notes** timeline.


---

## Managing Notes

Users can:

- View notes chronologically
- Search notes
- Edit notes
- Delete notes
- Pin important notes
- Add comments to notes
