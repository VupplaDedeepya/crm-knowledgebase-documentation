# Organization Details

The **Organization Details** page provides a complete view of an organization's information, relationships, and activity history. It serves as the central account workspace for managing B2B customer relationships by bringing together organization details, associated Contacts, Deals, and activities in a single location.

Users can use this page to:

- View organization information.
- Manage organization relationships.
- Access linked Contacts and Deals.
- Monitor account activity.
- Create new Contacts and Deals.
- Track all interactions associated with the Organization.

The Organization Details page helps sales teams maintain a consolidated view of customer accounts and their ongoing business activities.

---

## Organization Overview

The **Organization Overview** section displays key information about the Organization.

Typical information includes:

- Organization Name
- Owner
- Industry
- Company Size
- Revenue Band
- Website
- Primary Email
- Primary Phone
- Address Information
- Tags
- Notes

Additional fields may be displayed based on administrator-defined configurations and custom fields.


---

## Quick Actions

The **Organization Details** page provides quick access to common account management actions.

Available actions include:

- Edit Organization
- Add Contact
- Create Deal
- Log Activity

These actions allow users to manage the Organization without leaving the current page.


---

## Edit Organization

The **Edit Organization** action allows users to update Organization information whenever business details change.

### Steps

1. Open the required Organization record.
2. Click **Edit Organization**.
3. Update the required fields.
4. Click **Save**.

Changes are immediately reflected throughout the CRM.

---

### Editable Information

Users may update information such as:

- Organization Name
- Industry
- Website
- Contact Information
- Address Details
- Revenue Band
- Company Size
- Tags
- Notes

The fields available for editing depend on your organization's CRM configuration.

---

## Activity Timeline

The **Activity Timeline** provides a consolidated history of activities and updates associated with the Organization.

The timeline helps users understand account engagement and track important events without navigating between multiple records.

Activities are displayed in chronological order, with the most recent updates appearing first.

The timeline may include:

- Organization updates
- Contact updates
- Deal updates
- Tasks
- Meetings
- Notes
- System-generated activity logs


---

## Organization Detail Tabs

The Organization Details page includes dedicated tabs that allow users to manage related records and monitor account relationships.

### Linked Contacts

Displays all Contacts associated with the Organization, allowing users to view customer information and access Contact records directly.

---

### Linked Deals

Displays all Deals associated with the Organization, providing complete visibility into active and historical sales opportunities.

---

### Activities

Displays the chronological history of interactions, updates, tasks, meetings, notes, and other CRM activities related to the Organization.

## Relationship Integrity

Sales Astra CRM automatically maintains relationships between Organizations, Contacts, and Deals.

### Organization to Contact Relationship

- One Organization can have multiple Contacts.
- Each Contact belongs to a single Organization.

---

### Organization to Deal Relationship

- One Organization can have multiple Deals.
- Every Deal must be associated with a single Organization in B2B workflows.

This relationship ensures complete account-level visibility throughout the customer lifecycle.
