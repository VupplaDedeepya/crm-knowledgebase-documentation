# Activities Overview

The **Contact Activities** section helps users record, manage, and track every interaction associated with a Contact. It provides a centralized timeline of customer engagements, ensuring sales teams have complete visibility into communication history and follow-up activities.

Activities help maintain strong customer relationships by keeping all interactions organized within a single Contact record.


---

## Purpose of Contact Activities

The Contact Activities section helps organizations:

- Track customer interactions.
- Maintain communication history.
- Record follow-up activities.
- Improve collaboration among team members.
- Monitor customer engagement.
- Maintain a complete audit trail of customer interactions.

---

## Activity Timeline

All activities associated with a Contact are displayed in a chronological timeline.

The timeline provides a consolidated view of customer interactions, allowing users to quickly understand previous communications and planned follow-up actions.

Activities displayed in the timeline may include:

- Notes
- Tasks
- Meetings
- Emails
- Logged Activities
- Deal Creation
- Other CRM events


---

## Activity Filters

Users can quickly filter activities to display only specific activity types.

Available filters include:

- Notes
- Tasks
- Meetings
- Emails

Filtering activities helps users locate relevant interactions without scrolling through the complete activity timeline.

---

## Activity Management

Users can create and manage different types of activities directly from the Contact Activities section.

Available activity types include:

- Notes
- Tasks
- Meetings
- Emails

Each activity is automatically associated with the selected Contact and becomes part of the Contact's interaction history.

---

## Benefits

Using Contact Activities helps organizations:

- Maintain a complete customer engagement history.
- Improve follow-up management.
- Enhance collaboration across sales teams.
- Keep all customer interactions centralized.
- Improve visibility into customer relationships.
- Support better customer service and sales engagement.
