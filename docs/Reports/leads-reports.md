# Lead Reports

The **Lead Reports** section provides predefined reports that help users monitor Lead generation, qualification, ownership, conversion, and overall Lead performance.

These reports help sales teams and managers understand Lead trends, evaluate Lead sources, monitor Lead progress, and identify Leads that may require attention.

## Available Lead Reports

The following standard reports are available:

- **Lead Summary**
- **Lead Source Performance**
- **Lead Status Report**
- **Lead Owner Performance**
- **Lead Conversion Report**
- **Lead Aging Report**
- **Disqualified Leads Report**

---

## Lead Summary

The **Lead Summary** report provides an overview of Leads and their current lifecycle status.

![lead-summary](/img/reports/LeadSummary.png)

It can display metrics such as:

- Total Leads
- New Leads
- Qualified Leads
- Converted Leads
- Disqualified Leads
- Active Leads

The report provides both a visual representation and detailed Lead data.

---

## Lead Source Performance

The **Lead Source Performance** report helps users analyze Leads based on their source.

![Source-performance](/img/reports/SourcePerformance.png)

It can display:

- Lead Source
- Total Leads
- Qualified Leads
- Converted Leads
- Conversion Percentage

This report helps users compare the performance of different Lead sources.

---

## Lead Status Report

The **Lead Status Report** provides a breakdown of Leads based on their current status.

![status-performance](/img/reports/StatusPerformance.png)

For example:

- New
- Contacted
- Qualified
- Nurturing
- Disqualified
- Converted

The report helps users understand how Leads are distributed across the Lead lifecycle.

---

## Lead Owner Performance

The **Lead Owner Performance** report provides Lead performance information for individual owners.

It can display:

- Assigned Leads
- Qualified Leads
- Converted Leads
- Conversion Percentage

This allows managers to monitor Lead handling and conversion activity across owners.

![lead-owner-report](/img/reports/LeadOwnerReport.png)

---

## Lead Conversion Report

The **Lead Conversion Report** provides insights into Lead conversion performance.

![conversion-report](/img/reports/conversionReport.png)

It can display:

- Total Leads
- Converted Leads
- Conversion Rate
- Average Conversion Time

The report helps users understand how effectively Leads are progressing to conversion.

---

## Lead Aging Report

The **Lead Aging Report** groups Leads based on how long they have remained in the system.

![lead-aging-report](/img/reports/LeadAgingReport.png)

Common age groups include:

- 0–7 Days
- 8–15 Days
- 16–30 Days
- Over 30 Days

The report helps users identify Leads that have remained open for longer periods and may require follow-up.

---

## Disqualified Leads Report

The **Disqualified Leads Report** provides information about Leads that have been disqualified.

It can display:

- Lead Details
- Disqualification Reason
- Owner
- Date Disqualified

This helps users review disqualified Leads and understand the reasons for disqualification.

---

## Filtering Lead Reports

Lead reports can be filtered using the available report filters, such as:

- Date Range
- Lead Owner
- Team
- Region, where enabled
- Lead Source
- Lead Status
- Custom Fields, where applicable

Filters can be used to narrow the report results and focus on specific Lead data.

---

## Report Actions

Lead reports provide common reporting actions, including:

- **Sort** – Arrange report data based on available columns.
- **Pagination** – Navigate through multiple pages of report results.
- **Drill Down** – Open the corresponding Lead record from the report.
- **Export to Excel** – Export the report data to an Excel file.
- **Export to CSV** – Export the report data in CSV format.
- **Export to PDF** – Export the report in PDF format.
- **Schedule Report** – Configure scheduled delivery of the report where available.
