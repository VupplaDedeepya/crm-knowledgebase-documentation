# Creating and Managing Meetings

The **Meetings** feature allows CRM users to schedule and manage meetings with leads directly from the CRM system.

Meetings can be synchronized with external calendar providers such as:

- Google Calendar
- Microsoft Outlook
- Microsoft Exchange

This helps users track meetings inside the CRM while keeping their external calendars updated automatically.

---

## Meetings Tab

The Lead Details page contains a:

**Meetings**

tab under the Activities section.

Users can:

- Create meetings
- Log manual meetings
- View scheduled meetings
- Reschedule meetings
- Delete meetings

---

## Available Meeting Options

The Meetings section provides the following actions:

- Create Meeting
- Log Meeting

---

## Creating a Meeting

### Step 1: Open the Lead Record

- Navigate to the **Leads** Module.
- Open the required lead record.
- Go to the **Meetings** tab.

---

### Step 2: Click Create Meeting

- Click the **Add** button.

This opens the **Meeting Scheduling** flow.

![Create Meeting](/img/leads/CreateMeeting.png)

---

### Scheduling a Meeting

After calendar connection is completed, users can schedule meetings.

---

## Meeting Fields

| Field | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Event Title * | Title of the meeting |
| From * | User scheduling the meeting |
| Attendees * | Lead email and additional attendees |
| Start Date & Time * | Meeting start date and time |
| End Date & Time * | Meeting end date and time |
| Location * | Google Meet, Outlook Meet, or In Person |
| Description | Meeting agenda or notes |

---

### Attendees

The lead email is automatically added as an attendee.

Users can also:

- Add multiple users
- Add additional email addresses

---

### Calendar Slot Selection

Users can select meeting slots using the calendar view.

If existing events are already scheduled, they will be displayed to help avoid scheduling conflicts.

---

### Meeting Location Options

Users can select meeting locations such as:

- Google Meet
- Outlook Meet
- In Person

Online meeting links are automatically generated for supported providers.

This information is shared with all meeting attendees.

---

### Description Field

Users can enter:

- Meeting agenda
- Discussion topics
- Notes
- Follow-up details

---

## Saving the Meeting

After entering the required details:

- Click **Save**.

The meeting will:

- Sync to the connected calendar.
- Be added to the CRM activity timeline.
- Notify attendees.


---

## Rescheduling Meetings

Users can update:

- Meeting date
- Time slot
- Attendees

Updated information automatically syncs with the connected calendar.

---

## Logging Meetings

The **Log Meeting** feature allows users to manually record meetings that have already taken place with a lead.

This helps maintain accurate activity history, improves team visibility, and supports reporting and analytics within the CRM system.

Logged meetings are automatically added to:

- Lead Activity Timeline
- Meetings List View

---

## Accessing Log Meeting

### Steps to Open Log Meeting

- Navigate to the **Leads** Module.
- Open the required lead record.
- Go to the **Meetings** tab under Activities.
- Click **Log Meeting**.

This opens the **Log Meeting** form in a modal or side panel.

![Log Meeting](/img/leads/LogMeeting.png)

---

## Log Meeting Form Fields

The form captures structured meeting information for better tracking and reporting.

| Field | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Meeting Title * | Enter the title of the meeting |
| Meeting Type | Select meeting type |
| Date & Time of Meeting * | Select the meeting date and time |
| Participants | Add internal users and external participants |
| Summary * | Enter meeting discussion details |
| Outcome | Select the meeting outcome |
| Follow-up Task | Create a follow-up task if required |
| File Attachment | Upload supporting files or documents |

---

### Meeting Type Options

Users can select meeting types such as:

- Virtual
- In-Person
- Phone

---

### Virtual Meeting Providers

If the meeting type is selected as:

**Virtual**

users can choose providers such as:

- Microsoft Teams
- Google Meet
- Zoom Meeting

---

### Date and Time Selection

Users must select:

- Meeting date
- Meeting time

using the **Date-Time Picker**.

---

### Participants

Users can add:

- Internal CRM users
- External participant email addresses

This helps maintain complete attendee records for the meeting.

---

### Meeting Outcome

Users can select meeting outcomes such as:

- Successful
- Follow-up Required
- No Response
- No Show Up
- Rescheduled
- Cancelled

This helps track meeting effectiveness and follow-up requirements.

---

### File Attachments

Users can upload supporting files such as:

- Meeting documents
- Proposals
- Screenshots
- Notes

Attachments help maintain complete meeting records.


---

## Saving the Meeting

After entering all required details:

- Click **Submit**.

The meeting will be logged successfully.
