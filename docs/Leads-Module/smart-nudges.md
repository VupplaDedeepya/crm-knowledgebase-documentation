# Smart Nudges

## Overview

Smart Nudges are AI-generated recommendations that help sales teams take the most relevant next action for a Lead.

By analyzing Lead information, activity history, communication records, tasks, and engagement patterns, Smart Nudges provide actionable suggestions that help improve follow-up effectiveness and increase conversion opportunities.

Smart Nudges appear on the right side of the Lead Details page and are updated based on Lead activity and interactions.

---

## Benefits of Smart Nudges

Smart Nudges help sales teams:

- Prioritize important follow-up activities.
- Identify the next best action for a Lead.
- Reduce missed opportunities.
- Improve engagement with prospects.
- Maintain consistent follow-up processes.
- Increase productivity and response rates.

---

## Viewing Smart Nudges

Navigate to the desired Lead record and open the **Lead Details** page.

The **Smart Nudges** panel displays AI-generated recommendations along with supporting information.

Each nudge includes:

- Suggested Action
- Communication Channel
- Recommendation Description
- Priority Level

---

## Nudge Components

### Suggested Action

Displays the recommended activity that should be performed.

#### Examples

- Call Lead to Discuss Options
- Send Follow-Up Email
- Schedule Site Visit
- Share Product Brochure
- Book Product Demo

---

### Communication Channel

Indicates the preferred communication method for the suggested action.

Examples include:

- Email
- Call
- Meeting

---

### Recommendation Description

Provides additional context explaining why the action is being recommended.

#### Example

> "Call the Lead to discuss their apartment preferences and understand their requirements in more detail."

This helps sales representatives understand the purpose of the recommendation before taking action.

---

### Priority Levels

Each Smart Nudge includes a priority indicator to help users focus on the most important recommendations.
