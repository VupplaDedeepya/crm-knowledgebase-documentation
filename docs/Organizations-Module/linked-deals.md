# Linked Deals

The **Linked Deals** tab provides a centralized view of all Deals associated with an Organization. It enables users to monitor every sales opportunity related to a business account without navigating to the Deals module.

This helps sales teams understand the complete sales pipeline for an Organization, monitor deal progress, and track revenue opportunities from a single location.

---

## Overview

An Organization can have multiple Deals representing different business opportunities, projects, renewals, or sales engagements.

The **Linked Deals** tab serves as a central location for viewing and managing these opportunities throughout the sales lifecycle.

All Deals associated with the selected Organization are displayed, providing complete visibility into the account's revenue pipeline.


---

## Viewing Linked Deals

To view linked Deals:

1. Navigate to the **Organizations** module.
2. Open the required Organization record.
3. Select the **Linked Deals** tab.

The system displays all Deals associated with the Organization.

---

## Deal Summary

The Linked Deals page provides a summary of the Organization's sales opportunities.

The summary includes:

- **Total Linked Deals** – Total number of Deals associated with the Organization.
- **Open Deals** – Number of active Deals currently in progress.
- **Won Deals** – Number of successfully closed Deals.
- **Lost Deals** – Number of Deals that were not converted.
- **Total Pipeline Value** – Combined value of all linked Deals.

These metrics help users quickly evaluate the sales potential of the Organization.

---

## Deal Information

Each linked Deal displays important information, including:

- Deal Name
- Pipeline
- Stage
- Deal Value
- Deal Owner
- Expected Close Date
- Probability
- Deal Status

This information allows users to quickly review the progress and health of each sales opportunity.


---

## Opening a Deal

Each Deal includes a **View Deal** option.

Selecting **View Deal** opens the corresponding **Deal Details** page, where users can:

- View complete Deal information.
- Review Deal activities.
- Manage proposals and quotations.
- Track Deal progress.
- Monitor Deal scoring and smart nudges.
- Update the Deal stage.

---

## Creating a New Deal

Users can create a new Deal directly from the **Linked Deals** tab.

### Steps

1. Open the required Organization.
2. Navigate to the **Linked Deals** tab.
3. Click **Create Deal**.
4. Enter the required Deal information.
5. Click **Save**.

The newly created Deal is automatically associated with the selected Organization.


---

## Relationship Management

Sales Astra CRM automatically maintains the relationship between Organizations and Deals.

When a Deal is linked to an Organization:

- The Deal appears under the Organization's **Linked Deals** tab.
- The Organization is displayed in the Deal record.
- Changes to the Deal are reflected across the CRM.
- The relationship remains intact unless it is manually updated.

This ensures accurate account-level tracking throughout the sales lifecycle.

---

## Benefits

The **Linked Deals** tab helps users:

- View all Deals associated with an Organization.
- Monitor the complete sales pipeline for an account.
- Track revenue opportunities from a single location.
- Create new Deals directly from the Organization page.
- Access Deal records without leaving the Organization module.
- Maintain complete visibility into account-level sales activities.
