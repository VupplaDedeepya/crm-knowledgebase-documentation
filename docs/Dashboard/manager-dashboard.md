# Manager Dashboard

The **Manager Dashboard** provides sales managers with a centralized view of team pipeline performance, conversion, sales risks, individual sales performance, target achievement, and lead-source effectiveness.

It is designed to help managers **monitor their team's sales performance, identify pipeline risks, track progress against targets, and take timely action** from a single workspace.

The Manager Dashboard is assigned to users through **Role Management**. Users can also customize the Dashboard by showing or hiding available sections.

## Dashboard Sections

## 1. Pipeline Stats

The **Pipeline Stats** section provides a quick overview of the team's active sales pipeline.

![pipeline-stats](/img/dashboard/PipelineStats.png)

### Metrics Displayed

- **Total Pipeline Value** – Total value of active Deals in the pipeline.
- **Total Active Deals** – Number of active Deals.
- **Average Deal Size** – Average value of active Deals.

The section also displays the number of pipeline stages represented in the current data.

## 2. Stage Distribution

The **Stage Distribution** section shows how the team's pipeline value is distributed across Deal stages.

![stage-distribution](/img/dashboard/StageDistribution.png)

The visualization provides a breakdown of:

- Deal Stage
- Number of Deals
- Pipeline Value
- Percentage of Total Pipeline

Examples of stages may include:

- Opportunity
- Discovery
- Property Recommendation
- Site Visit Scheduled
- Site Visit Completed
- Pricing Discussion
- Booking Commitment
- Other

Managers can use this information to understand where Deals are concentrated within the pipeline.

Clicking a stage can open the corresponding filtered Deal records.

## 3. Pipeline by Rep

The **Pipeline by Rep** section provides a sales representative-level view of the active pipeline.

![pipeline-by-rep](/img/dashboard/PipelineByRep.png)

### Information Displayed

- Sales Representative
- Pipeline Share %
- Number of Deals
- Pipeline Amount

The section allows managers to identify how pipeline value is distributed across individual sales representatives.

Clicking a representative can provide access to the relevant performance or Deal information, subject to permissions.

## 4. Conversion Funnel

The **Conversion Funnel** provides a visual representation of the team's sales conversion process.

![conversion-funnel](/img/dashboard/ConversionFunnel.png)

The funnel can show progression through stages such as:

**Leads → Qualified Leads → Deals → Won**

Managers can use the funnel to understand:

- Number of Leads entering the funnel
- Number of Qualified Leads
- Number of Deals created
- Number of Won Deals
- Drop-off between stages

This helps provide a high-level view of conversion performance.

## 5. Risk & Alerts

The **Risk & Alerts** section identifies Deals or sales conditions that may require manager attention.

![risks-&-alerts](/img/dashboard/Risks&Alerts.png)

Managers can view alerts across categories such as:

- All Alerts
- Deals
- Activity
- Rep Risks

### Example Alerts

- Stage stuck
- Close date passed
- Deal inactivity
- Aging Deal
- Pipeline risk
- Sales representative risk

Each alert may display:

- Record or sales representative
- Number of risks detected
- Risk type
- Priority
- Age or duration
- Alert time

### Available Actions

Depending on the alert, managers can:

- **View** – Open the relevant record or details.
- **Assign** – Assign the item to a user.
- **Remind** – Create or trigger a reminder for follow-up.

## 6. Team Performance Leaderboard

The **Team Performance Leaderboard** provides a comparative view of sales representative performance within the manager's team.

![team-performance](/img/dashboard/TeamPerformance.png)

The leaderboard can display:

- Rank
- Sales Representative
- Revenue
- Number of Deals
- Conversion %
- Activities Completed

Managers can select the metric used for ranking, such as **Revenue Closed**.

The section may also highlight the top performers separately for quick visibility.

:::note
Rankings are calculated using the selected performance metric and reporting period.
:::

## 7. Target Achievement

The **Target Achievement** section compares sales performance against configured targets.

The visualization can compare target and achieved values for relevant Deals, teams, or business units.

![target-achievement](/img/dashboard/TargetAchievement.png)

Managers can use this section to monitor:

- Target value
- Achieved value
- Achievement percentage
- Performance against target

The available data depends on the configured targets and the user's access permissions.

## 8. Source Performance Analytics

The **Source Performance Analytics** section provides performance information based on Lead Source.

![source-performance-analytics](/img/dashboard/SourcePerformanceAnalytics.png)

### Information Displayed

- Source
- Total Leads
- Qualified Leads
- Won Deals
- Conversion %
- Revenue

Examples of Lead Sources may include:

- Facebook
- Website
- Social Media
- Email Campaign
- Instagram
- Other configured sources

This allows managers to understand how different Lead Sources contribute to the sales pipeline and revenue.
