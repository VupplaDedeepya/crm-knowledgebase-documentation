### Overview

The **Leads** module enables users to capture and manage potential customers within CX Astra CRM. Creating accurate lead records helps sales teams organize prospects, track interactions, and streamline the sales process.

The Lead Creation form is fully configurable by CRM administrators. Depending on your organization's configuration, the form may include different sections, custom fields, validation rules, and business-specific information.

---

### Purpose

Creating lead records allows your organization to:

- Capture enquiries from potential customers.
- Organize prospect information in a centralized location.
- Assign ownership to sales representatives.
- Track lead qualification throughout the sales cycle.
- Prepare leads for conversion into deals.

---

### Before You Begin

Before creating a lead, ensure that:

- You have permission to create Lead records.
- Required fields are available based on your organization's Lead Configuration.
- Any mandatory business information is available.

:::note
The fields displayed in the Lead Creation form depend on your organization's **Lead Configuration** settings. Custom fields and validation rules may vary across organizations.
:::

---

### Accessing the Lead Creation Form

To create a new Lead:

1. Log in to **CX Astra CRM**.
2. Navigate to **Leads** from the left navigation menu.
3. Click **Create Lead**.

The Lead Creation form opens.

![Lead Creation Form](/img/leads/LeadCreation.png)

---

### Enter Lead Information

The Lead Creation form displays the fields configured by your CRM administrator.

Depending on your organization's configuration, the form may contain the following sections.

| Section | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Basic Information | Captures the Lead's name and general details. |
| Contact Information | Stores email address, phone number, and communication details. |
| Company Information | Records company-related information. |
| Lead Management | Defines Lead Owner, Status, Source, and other business fields. |
| Address Details | Stores location and address information. |
| Social Information | Stores social media profiles and website details. |

---

### Field Requirements

While entering Lead information, keep the following in mind:

- Mandatory fields are marked with an asterisk (**\***).
- Validation rules are enforced automatically.
- Dropdowns display administrator-configured values.
- Lookup fields allow linking existing CRM records.
- Custom fields may appear depending on your organization's configuration.

:::tip
Complete as much information as possible while creating a Lead to improve qualification accuracy and future reporting.
:::

---

### Additional Information

Depending on your organization's configuration, you may also be able to:

- Upload files or images.
- Add notes or descriptions.
- Apply Tags.
- Assign a Lead Owner.
- Select the Lead Status.
- Capture organization-specific information using custom fields.

---

### Save the Lead

After entering the required information:

1. Review all entered details.
2. Click **Create Lead**.

If all validations are successful:

- The Lead is created successfully.
- A confirmation message is displayed.
- The Lead becomes available in the Leads list.

---

### Example

A sales representative receives an enquiry from a customer through the company website.

The representative creates a Lead with the following information:

| Field | Value |
|---------|--------|
| Lead Name | John Smith |
| Company | ABC Technologies |
| Email | john.smith@example.com |
| Phone | +1 9876543210 |
| Lead Source | Website |
| Lead Owner | Sarah Johnson |
| Status | New |

After saving, the Lead becomes available for qualification and follow-up.

---

### Important Notes

:::note

- The Lead Creation form is dynamically generated based on administrator configuration.
- Custom fields are automatically displayed when configured.
- Field validations are enforced according to organizational policies.
- Changes made in **Lead Configuration** are immediately reflected in the Lead Creation form.

:::

---

### Best Practices

- Complete all available Lead information whenever possible.
- Assign the correct Lead Owner during creation.
- Apply Tags for easier segmentation.
- Verify contact details before saving.
- Use meaningful Lead Sources for accurate reporting.
- Avoid creating duplicate Lead records.
