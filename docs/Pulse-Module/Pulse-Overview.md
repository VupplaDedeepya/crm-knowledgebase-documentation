# Pulse Inbox

## Overview

**Pulse Inbox** is a centralized workspace for managing customer conversations across multiple channels such as **Pulse, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger**. It allows agents to view conversations, respond to customers, assign priorities, create leads, add internal notes, and manage conversation outcomes from a single screen.

---

## Pulse Inbox Layout

The **Pulse Inbox** consists of three main sections.

### Conversation List Panel

Located on the left side of the screen, this panel displays all customer conversations.

Each conversation displays:

- Channel icon
- Customer name or identifier
- Priority
- Current status
- Assigned user initials (if assigned)
- Latest message preview
- Timestamp

Users can switch between channels using:

- All Chat
- Pulse
- WhatsApp
- Instagram
- Messenger

![Conversation List Panel](/img/Pulse-Inbox/ConversationListPanel.png)

---

## Conversation Thread Panel

The center panel displays the complete conversation history.

This section allows users to:

- View customer messages
- View agent responses
- Send replies
- Attach files
- Add internal notes
- Generate AI summaries
- Create leads
- End conversations

![Conversation Thread Panel](/img/Pulse-Inbox/ConversationThreadPanel.png)

---

## Contact Information Panel

The right panel displays customer and conversation details.

### Contact Information

- Contact Name
- Email Address
- Mobile Number

### Conversation Details

- Status
- Assigned Agent
- Channel
- First Message Date
- Priority

### AI Summary

Displays a generated summary of the conversation when available.

---

## Searching and Filtering Conversations

Pulse Inbox provides multiple options for locating conversations.

### Search

Use the search bar to quickly find conversations.

Users can search using:

- Customer name
- Mobile number
- Email address
- Conversation keywords

Search results update dynamically as text is entered.


---

### Filters

Conversations can be filtered using:

- Read
- Last Activity
- Tag
- Priority
- Assignee

Additional filter options can be accessed using the **Filters** icon.

---

## Conversation Views

Users can switch between:

- All
- Assigned
- Unassigned
- Not Responded

Conversations can also be grouped by:

- New
- In Progress
- Completed
- All


---

## Managing Conversation Priority

Each conversation can be assigned a priority level to indicate urgency.

Priority levels include:

- High
- Medium
- Low

#@# To update priority

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click the **Priority** selector in the conversation footer.
3. Select the required priority.

The updated priority is displayed in both the conversation list and the conversation details.

---

## Internal Notes

Internal notes allow agents to capture information related to a conversation for internal reference.

Notes are visible only to internal users and are not shared with customers.

### Common Uses

- Recording follow-up actions
- Capturing qualification details
- Sharing context with team members
- Storing important observations


---

## Creating a Lead

When a conversation represents a potential sales opportunity, a lead can be created directly from **Pulse Inbox**.

### To create a lead

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Create Lead**.
3. Complete the required information.
4. Click **Save**.

After successful creation:

- The conversation displays the **Lead Created** status.
- Lead information becomes available in the **Contact Information** panel.
- Users can access the lead record through **View in CRM**.

---

## AI Summary

Pulse Inbox can generate an AI-powered summary of a conversation.

The summary helps users quickly understand:

- Customer intent
- Topics discussed
- Key information collected
- Suggested next steps

To generate a summary, click **Generate AI Summary**.

---

## Marking a Conversation as Insufficient Data

If a conversation does not contain enough information for further qualification or follow-up, it can be marked as **Insufficient Data**.

This option is available from:

- More Actions menu
- Quick Actions panel

---

# Marking a Conversation as Junk

Conversations that are irrelevant, spam, or do not require further action can be marked as **Junk**.

This option is available from:

- More Actions menu
- Quick Actions panel


---

## Ending a Conversation

Agents can close a conversation when no further interaction is required.

### To end a conversation

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **End Conversation**.

The option is available from:

- Conversation footer
- More Actions menu
- Quick Actions panel

Once ended, the conversation is treated as closed until a new customer message is received.

---

## More Actions Menu

The **More Actions (⋮)** menu provides quick access to conversation actions.

Available actions include:

- Assign
- Priority
- Insufficient Data
- Mark as Junk
- Unassign

For conversations that have not yet been converted into leads, the menu may also display:

- Create Lead

---

## Viewing Lead Information

For conversations that have been converted into leads, the **Contact Information** panel displays additional lead-related information such as:

- Lead Status
- Lead Score

Users can click **View in CRM** to open the corresponding lead record for further management.


---

## Benefits

The Pulse Inbox helps users:

- Manage conversations from multiple channels in one place.
- Respond to customers efficiently.
- Prioritize conversations based on urgency.
- Generate AI-powered conversation summaries.
- Create leads directly from conversations.
- Add internal notes for team collaboration.
- Track conversation status and ownership.
- Access CRM records without leaving the conversation.
- Improve response times and customer engagement.
