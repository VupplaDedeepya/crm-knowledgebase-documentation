# Leads Overview

The **Leads** module in Sales Astra CRM is designed to help organizations capture, organize, and manage potential customers throughout the early stages of the sales process. It serves as a centralized workspace where sales teams can maintain lead information, track interactions, prioritize opportunities, and prepare qualified prospects for conversion into Deals.

Whether Leads are generated through websites, marketing campaigns, referrals, social media, or manual entry, the Leads module provides all the tools required to efficiently manage them from initial enquiry to qualification.

---

## What You Can Do with the Leads Module

The Leads module provides a comprehensive set of features to simplify Lead management, including:

- Create and manage Lead records.
- Store customer, company, and contact information.
- Organize Leads using statuses, tags, owners, and custom fields.
- Search and filter Leads using powerful search and advanced filtering options.
- Create personalized Custom Views to quickly access frequently used Lead data.
- Perform Bulk Actions on multiple Lead records simultaneously.
- Record every customer interaction through Notes, Tasks, Meetings, and Emails.
- Receive AI-powered Smart Nudges that recommend the next best action.
- Convert qualified Leads into Deals while preserving activity history.
- Export Lead records for reporting, analysis, and business intelligence.

---

## Lead Management Capabilities

The Leads module helps sales teams manage the complete Lead lifecycle by providing tools for:

- Capturing Lead information.
- Tracking Lead progress through different statuses.
- Assigning Leads to sales representatives.
- Scheduling follow-up activities.
- Collaborating with team members.
- Monitoring customer engagement.
- Prioritizing high-value prospects.
- Maintaining a complete interaction history.

---

## Lead Activities

Every Lead maintains a complete activity timeline, allowing users to record and manage interactions throughout the sales process.

Users can:

- Create Notes
- Create Tasks
- Schedule Meetings
- Log Meetings
- Send Emails
- Track communication history

This centralized timeline ensures that every interaction is available for future reference and collaboration.

Sales Astra CRM enhances Lead management by providing intelligent features that improve sales productivity and decision-making.
