# Emails Management

The **Emails** feature allows users to compose, send, schedule, and track emails directly from the Deals module. All email communications are automatically associated with the corresponding Deal, providing a centralized view of customer interactions throughout the sales process.

This eliminates the need to switch between external email applications and the CRM while ensuring complete communication history is maintained.

---

## Accessing Emails

1. Navigate to the **Deals** module.
2. Open the required Deal record.
3. Go to the **Emails** tab under the **Activities** section.
4. Click **Compose Email**.

This opens the **Email Composer**.

![Compose Email](/img/deals/DealComposeEmail.png)

---

## Email Composer

The Email Composer allows users to create professional emails using rich formatting tools.

Users can:

- Enter recipients.
- Add a subject.
- Compose the email body.
- Format text.
- Attach files.
- Insert templates.
- Schedule emails.
- Save drafts.

---

## Email Templates

Users can insert predefined email templates to simplify communication and maintain consistency.

Templates help users:

- Save time.
- Standardize communication.
- Reduce repetitive typing.
- Improve customer engagement.

---

## Insert Options

The Email Composer supports inserting:

- Email Templates
- CRM Documents
- Meeting Links
- Quotes

These items can be added directly into the email body.

---

## Attachments

Users can attach supporting files while composing an email.

Examples include:

- Proposal Documents
- Quotations
- Product Brochures
- Images
- PDFs
- Other supporting documents

---

## Send Options

Users can choose one of the following options:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Send Now | Sends the email immediately. |
| Schedule Send | Schedules the email for a future date and time. |
| Save Draft | Saves the email for future editing. |

---

## Schedule Send

The **Schedule Send** option allows users to specify the exact date and time when an email should be delivered.

Scheduled emails remain in the CRM until the configured delivery time.

---

## Save Draft

If an email is not ready to be sent, users can save it as a draft.

Draft emails can be edited and sent later.

---

## Email Status

The CRM tracks the status of each email.

Common statuses include:

- Draft
- Scheduled
- Sent
- Delivered
- Failed

These statuses help users monitor email communication with customers.

---

## Email Activity Timeline

All emails sent from the Deals module are automatically recorded in the **Deal Activity Timeline**.

Each email entry displays information such as:

- Subject
- Sender
- Recipients
- Date and Time
- Delivery Status

This provides complete visibility into communication history for the Deal.

---

## Best Practices

- Use email templates for consistent communication.
- Review recipients before sending emails.
- Attach relevant documents when required.
- Schedule follow-up emails for important opportunities.
- Monitor email statuses to ensure successful delivery.
- Maintain complete communication history within the Deal record.
