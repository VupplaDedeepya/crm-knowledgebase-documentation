# Deal Details

The **Deal Details** page provides a centralized view of all information related to a specific Deal. It enables users to track Deal progress, manage activities, monitor Deal performance, collaborate with stakeholders, and take actions that help move opportunities through the sales pipeline.

Users can access a Deal's complete history, communication records, tasks, meetings, score insights, and AI-powered recommendations from a single location.

---

## Deal Information

The left panel displays the key details associated with the Deal.

Depending on your organization's configuration, the following information may be available:

- Deal Title
- Account (Organization)
- Contact
- Pipeline
- Stage
- Deal Value
- Currency
- Status
- Deal Owner
- Tags
- Deal Score

> **Note**
>
> The fields displayed in the Deal Details section may vary based on the Deal Form configuration defined by your CRM administrator.

---

## Deal Stage Management

The **Deal Stage** section visually represents the current position of the Deal within the selected sales pipeline.

Each stage represents a milestone in the sales process and helps users understand the progress of the opportunity.

---

## Viewing the Current Stage

The currently active stage is highlighted within the pipeline progression bar.

### Examples of stages may include:

- Qualification
- Property Matched
- Site Visit Scheduled
- Site Visit Completed
- Negotiation/Review
- Closed Won
- Closed Lost

In some pipelines, stages may also display probability percentages that indicate the likelihood of successfully closing the Deal.

> **Note**
>
> Available stages depend on the pipeline configuration defined by your CRM administrator.

---

## Moving a Deal to Another Stage

As the opportunity progresses, users can update the Deal stage to reflect the latest status.

### Steps

1. Open the required Deal record.
2. Navigate to the **Insights** tab.
3. Click **Move the Stage**.
4. Select the desired stage from the available list.

![Deal Stages](/img/deals/DealStages.png)

The Deal is automatically updated within the pipeline.

---

## Benefits

- Maintains accurate pipeline visibility.
- Improves sales forecasting.
- Enables stage-based automation.
- Provides better reporting and analytics.

---

## Marking a Deal as Won

When a Deal is successfully closed, users can mark it as **Won**.

### Steps

1. Open the Deal record.
2. Click **Won**.
3. Confirm the action if prompted.

Once marked as **Won**:

- The Deal is considered successfully closed.
- Revenue reporting is updated accordingly.
- Won Deal metrics are reflected in reports and dashboards.

---

## Marking a Deal as Lost

When an opportunity is no longer being pursued, users can mark it as **Lost**.

### Steps

1. Open the Deal record.
2. Click **Lost**.
3. Confirm the action if prompted.

Once marked as **Lost**:

- The Deal is removed from active sales forecasting.
- Loss metrics become available for reporting and analysis.
