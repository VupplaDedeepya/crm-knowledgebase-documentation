# Calendar

The **Calendar** module provides a visual scheduling interface for managing CRM activities. It displays tasks and meetings in a calendar format, allowing users to plan work, monitor upcoming commitments, and track activity schedules across **Leads, Deals, Contacts, and Organizations**.

All activities created within the CRM are automatically reflected in the Calendar, providing a centralized view of scheduled work.

![Calendar Overview](/img/Calendar/Calendar.png)

---

## Calendar Views

The Calendar supports multiple viewing modes to accommodate different planning needs.

### Day View

Displays activities scheduled for a single day.

This view is useful for managing daily workloads and appointments.

![Day View](/img/Calendar/DayView.png)

---

### Week View

Displays activities across an entire week.

This view helps users plan upcoming tasks, meetings, and follow-ups over several days.

![Week View](/img/Calendar/WeekView.png)

---

### Month View

Displays activities for the entire month.

This view provides a high-level overview of upcoming commitments and workload distribution.

![Month View](/img/Calendar/MonthView.png)

---

## Calendar Sidebar

The left-hand panel provides quick access to scheduling information.

### Mini Calendar

Allows users to quickly jump to a specific date.

---

### Today's Meetings

Displays meetings scheduled for the current day.

This section helps users quickly identify upcoming commitments without navigating through the calendar.


---

## Creating Meetings

Users can schedule meetings directly from the Calendar module.

### Steps

1. Navigate to **Calendar**.
2. Click **New**.
3. Enter the meeting details.
4. Select the related CRM record (**Lead, Deal, Contact, or Organization**, if applicable).
5. Specify the meeting date and time.
6. Click **Save**.

Once created, the meeting is automatically added to:

- The Calendar view.
- The Global Activities section.
- The associated Lead, Deal, Contact, or Organization record.

This ensures that all participants and related records have visibility into the scheduled meeting.

![Create Meeting](/img/Calendar/CreateMeeting.png)

## Benefits

The Calendar module helps users:

- Visualize scheduled meetings and activities.
- Plan daily, weekly, and monthly workloads.
- Keep track of upcoming commitments.
- Schedule meetings directly from a centralized interface.
- Access meetings associated with Leads, Deals, Contacts, and Organizations.
- Improve collaboration and time management across the sales team.
