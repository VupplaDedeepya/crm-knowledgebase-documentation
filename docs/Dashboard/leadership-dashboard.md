# Leadership Dashboard

The **Leadership Dashboard** provides business leaders, executives, and administrators with a consolidated view of organizational sales performance, revenue, pipeline health, forecasts, conversion metrics, strategic insights, and business risks.

It is designed to provide a **high-level business overview** that helps leadership monitor organizational performance, identify risks and trends, and make informed decisions without navigating through multiple reports or modules.

The Leadership Dashboard can be assigned to a role by an administrator through **Role Management**. Users can also customize the Dashboard by showing or hiding available sections.

## Dashboard Sections

## 1. Sales Forecast

The **Sales Forecast** section provides an outlook of expected revenue for the selected period.

It helps leadership compare the organization's target with expected revenue and understand whether the current pipeline provides sufficient coverage to achieve the target.

![sales-forecast](/img/dashboard/SalesForecast.png)

### Information Displayed

- **Target** – Revenue target for the selected period.
- **Expected Revenue** – Forecasted revenue based on the current pipeline.
- **Pipeline Coverage** – Pipeline coverage relative to the target.
- **Forecast Gap** – Difference between expected revenue and the target.
- **Forecast Status** – Indicates the current forecast position.
- **Forecast Breakdown by Stage** – Shows the distribution of forecast value across early, mid, and late stages.

## 2. Conversion Metrics

The **Conversion Metrics** section provides an overview of the organization's sales funnel conversion performance.

It helps leadership understand how effectively records are progressing from leads to deals and from deals to won business.

![conversion-metrics](/img/dashboard/ConversionMetrics.png)

### Metrics Include

- Lead → Deal Conversion
- Deal → Won Conversion
- Lead → Won Conversion

Each metric can display the current conversion percentage and its change compared with the previous period.

### Funnel View

A funnel visualization displays the progression of records through key stages, such as:

**Leads → Deals → Won**

## 3. Pipeline Health

The **Pipeline Health** section provides an overview of the quality and distribution of the active sales pipeline.

![pipeline-health](/img/dashboard/PipelineHealth.png)

### Information Displayed

- Total Pipeline Value
- Number of Active Deals
- Average Deal Size
- Stage Distribution
- Pipeline Coverage
- Aging Breakdown

### Aging Breakdown

Deals can be grouped based on how long they have remained in the pipeline, for example:

- 0–15 days
- 15–30 days
- 30+ days

The section can also identify deals that may require attention because they have remained in the pipeline for an extended period.

## 4. Strategic Insights

The **Strategic Insights** section highlights important changes, trends, and potential areas requiring management attention.

![strategic-insights](/img/dashboard/StrategicInsights.png)

Insights can be viewed across categories such as:

- All Alerts
- Deals
- Activity
- Rep Risks

Examples of insights may include:

- Changes in conversion performance
- Revenue growth or decline
- Aging pipeline concentration
- Stuck deals
- Sales representative risks

Each insight can provide a **View Details** option to access additional information.

## 5. Revenue Overview

The **Revenue Overview** section provides a consolidated view of organizational revenue performance.

![Revenue-Overview](/img/dashboard/RevenueOverview.png)

### Revenue KPIs

The section can display:

- Revenue (MTD) – Month-to-date revenue
- Revenue (QTD) – Quarter-to-date revenue
- Revenue (YTD) – Year-to-date revenue
- Average Deal Value

The metrics can include comparisons with the previous month, quarter, or year.

### Revenue Progress

The **Revenue Progress** component compares actual revenue against the configured target.

It displays:

- Revenue
- Target
- Achievement %
- Remaining Target

A progress indicator provides a visual representation of target achievement.

### Revenue Breakdown

Revenue can be categorized into:

- Won Revenue
- Open Pipeline
- Lost Revenue

### Revenue Trend

The **Revenue Trend** component provides a time-based view of revenue performance.

The trend can be viewed using available time periods such as:

- Week
- Month
- Quarter
- Year

## 6. Sales Performance

The **Sales Performance** section provides a high-level view of sales performance across teams and sales users.

![sales-performance](/img/dashboard/SalesPerformance.png)

### Top Teams

Displays teams with their relevant performance information, such as:

- Team Name
- Won Deals
- Revenue
- Contribution percentage
- Performance trend

### Revenue vs Target Achievement

A visual comparison of:

- Revenue
- Target achievement

can be displayed for the selected teams or business units.

### Top Performers

The section can display the leading sales performers based on available sales performance metrics.

:::note
The displayed rankings are based on the selected performance metric and reporting period.
:::

## 7. Business Risks

The **Business Risks** section highlights business conditions that may require leadership attention.

It provides prioritized risk information along with supporting metrics.

![business-risks](/img/dashboard/BusinessRisks.png)

### Example Risk Categories

- Revenue target risk
- Pipeline risk
- Aging pipeline
- Stalled deals
- Conversion risk
- Sales activity risk

Each risk can display:

- Risk priority
- Risk title
- Affected area
- Supporting metric
- Gap or variance
- Trend
- Recommended action

### Recommendations

Where applicable, the Dashboard may provide recommended actions related to the identified business risk.

For example, a revenue target risk may include a recommendation to increase pipeline generation or focus on late-stage opportunities.
