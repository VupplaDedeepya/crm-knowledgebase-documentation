# Export Leads

The **Export Leads** feature allows users to download Lead records from the CRM for reporting, analysis, data sharing, or backup purposes.

---

## Accessing Export Leads

1. Navigate to the **Leads** module.
2. Apply filters or select a Custom View if you want to export specific records.
3. Click the **Export** button available in the top-right corner of the Leads page.


---

## Export Options

Users can export:

- **All Leads** – Exports all Lead records available in the selected view.
- **Filtered Leads** – Exports only Leads matching the applied filters.
- **Selected Leads** – Exports only the Leads selected using checkboxes and the Bulk Actions toolbar.

---

## Information Included in the Export

Depending on the selected columns and system configuration, exported files may include:

- Lead Name
- Lead ID
- Lead Status
- Contact Information
- Email Address
- Company Details
- Tags
- Lead Owner
- Creation Date
- Last Activity Date

---

## Common Use Cases

### Reporting and Analysis

Export Lead data for sales performance reviews, pipeline analysis, and management reporting.

---

### Data Sharing

Share Lead information with team members, managers, or external stakeholders when required.

---

### Data Backup

Maintain an offline copy of Lead records for audit and backup purposes.

---

### Marketing and Campaign Planning

Analyze Lead segments and campaign performance using external tools such as spreadsheets or business intelligence platforms.

---

## Best Practices

- Apply filters before exporting to ensure only relevant records are included.
- Verify selected columns to avoid exporting unnecessary data.
- Review exported files before sharing them externally.
- Ensure compliance with your organization's data privacy and security policies when handling exported Lead information.
