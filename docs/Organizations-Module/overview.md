# Organizations Overview

The **Organizations** module in Sales Astra CRM serves as the central repository for managing companies, businesses, and customer accounts throughout the sales lifecycle. In a B2B environment, an Organization represents the company or account with which your sales team interacts, providing a single source of truth for all account-related information.

An Organization acts as the parent entity for Contacts, Deals, and account-level activities, enabling sales teams to maintain a complete view of customer relationships, business interactions, and revenue opportunities from a single location.

By maintaining Organizations as separate records, Sales Astra CRM helps eliminate duplicate company information, improves account visibility, and strengthens relationship management across the entire customer lifecycle. 

---

## What You Can Do with the Organizations Module

The Organizations module provides a comprehensive set of features to help users efficiently manage business accounts, including:

- Create and manage Organization records.
- Associate multiple Contacts with an Organization.
- Link multiple Deals to an Organization.
- Maintain company and business information.
- Track account-level activities and interactions.
- Perform Bulk Actions on multiple Organizations.
- Import and Export Organization records.
- Create personalized Custom Views.
- Search and filter Organization records.
- Monitor account relationships and business opportunities.
- Access linked Contacts and Deals from a single account.

## Relationship Management

Organizations act as the central account record that connects multiple CRM entities.

Users can:

- Associate multiple Contacts with an Organization.
- Link multiple Deals to an Organization.
- View all Contacts belonging to a company.
- Monitor all business opportunities associated with an account.
- Maintain complete account-level visibility throughout the sales lifecycle.

This interconnected structure provides a unified view of customer accounts, helping sales teams better understand customer relationships and business engagement.

---

## Account Activities

Each Organization maintains a centralized history of activities and business interactions.

Users can:

- Create Contacts.
- Create Deals.
- Log Activities.
- View the Activity Timeline.
- Track account updates.
- Monitor customer engagement across the organization.

This centralized activity history helps teams collaborate effectively while maintaining complete visibility into account-level interactions.
