# Quotes Management

The **Quotes** functionality in the Deals Module helps sales teams create, upload, manage, send, and track customer-facing quotation documents directly within a Deal.

Quotes help organizations maintain pricing transparency, standardize commercial communication, and centralize quotation management throughout the Deal lifecycle.

---

## What is a Quote?

A **Quote** is a commercial pricing document shared with customers containing:

- Product or service pricing
- Quantity
- Taxes
- Discounts
- Commercial terms
- Validity period

Quotes are mainly used during pricing discussions and negotiations.

---

## Importance of Quotes

Quotes help organizations:

- Share accurate pricing.
- Improve pricing transparency.
- Maintain quotation history.
- Reduce pricing errors.
- Track customer approvals.

---

## Accessing Quotes

Quotes can be accessed from the **Deal Details** page under the **Quotes** tab.


---

## Creating a Quote

### Step 1: Open Quotes Tab

- Navigate to the **Deals** module.
- Open the required Deal.
- Go to the **Quotes** tab.

---

### Step 2: Click Add

- Click the **Add** button.

This opens the **Quote Creation Options** window.



---

### Quote Creation Options

The CRM provides multiple methods for quote management.

![Quote Options](/img/Deals/QuoteOptions.png)

## Option 1: Upload Quote

This option allows users to upload an already created quotation document.

### Steps

1. Click **Upload Quote**.
2. Upload the quotation document.
3. Add the required details.
4. Save the quote.

![upload-quote](/img/deals/UploadQuote.png)

---

### Upload Quote Features

Users can:

- Upload quotation files.
- Associate quotes with Deals.
- Track uploaded quotations.
- Maintain centralized quotation history.

---

### Supported File Formats

The CRM supports:

- PDF
- DOC
- DOCX

> **Note**
>
> The maximum file size may vary depending on your organization's configuration.

---

## Option 2: Upload & Send via CXAstra

This option allows users to upload and directly send quotations using CRM communication channels.

### Features

- Direct customer sharing.
- Email tracking.
- Customer engagement monitoring.
- Centralized communication management.

![Send-Via-CXAstra](/img/deals/QuoteSendViaCXAstra.png)

---

## Option 3: Create Quote (Quick)

This option allows users to quickly generate quotations directly within the CRM.

### Steps

1. Click **Create Quote**.
2. Add products or services.
3. Enter quantity and pricing.
4. Apply taxes and discounts.
5. Save the quote.

![Quote-Template](/img/deals/QuoteTemplate.png)

---

## Quote Validations

The CRM validates the following before a quote is created or shared:

- Quote currency must match the Deal currency.
- Discounts must follow organization policies.
- Validity date is mandatory.

---

## Discount Approval Rules

If discounts exceed the permitted limits, the CRM may require manager approval before the quote can be approved or shared with the customer.

---

## Quote Lifecycle

Quotes generally move through the following stages:

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Draft | Quote created. |
| Pending Approval | Waiting for approval. |
| Approved | Approved for customer sharing. |
| Sent | Shared with the customer. |
| Accepted | Customer accepted the quote. |
| Rejected | Customer rejected the quote. |
| Expired | Quote validity expired. |

---

## Quote Tracking

The CRM tracks quotation activities throughout the Deal lifecycle.

This includes:

- Quote uploads.
- Quote sharing history.
- Status updates.
- Customer responses.
- Activity timeline records.

---

## Activity Timeline Integration

All quote activities are automatically recorded in the **Deal Activity Timeline**.

This includes:

- Uploads.
- Status changes.
- Sharing activities.
- Approval updates.
- Customer interactions.

This provides complete visibility into the quotation lifecycle directly from the Deal record.

---

## AI and Smart Insights

Sales Astra CRM analyzes quotation activities to provide valuable insights, including:

- Deal Scoring.
- Engagement Insights.
- Smart Nudges.
- Follow-up Recommendations.

Customer interactions with quotations help the CRM identify buying intent, measure customer engagement, and recommend appropriate next actions.

---

## Best Practices

- Verify pricing before sharing quotations.
- Ensure discounts comply with organizational policies.
- Keep quotation statuses updated throughout the sales process.
- Review customer responses regularly.
- Monitor quotation validity dates to avoid sending expired quotes.
- Use Activity Timeline and Smart Nudges to track engagement and plan follow-up actions.
