# Smart Nudges

The **Smart Nudges** panel provides AI-powered recommendations that help users take the most effective next actions for a Deal.

These recommendations are generated based on available Deal data, activity history, engagement patterns, stage progression, and scoring insights.

---

## Purpose of Smart Nudges

Smart Nudges help users:

- Prioritize important actions.
- Identify Deal risks early.
- Improve buyer engagement.
- Maintain sales momentum.
- Increase conversion rates.
- Make informed decisions throughout the sales cycle.

---

## Nudge Priority Levels

Each recommendation is assigned a priority level.

- Low
- Medium
- High

---

## Example Smart Nudges

Depending on Deal activity and engagement, users may receive recommendations such as:

### Confirm Client's Decision-Making Timeline

Encourages users to understand the client's approval process and expected decision timeframe.

---

### Address Budget Concerns Proactively

Suggests discussing pricing concerns, alternative options, or budget-related questions before they become obstacles.

> **Note**
>
> Smart Nudges are dynamic and may vary based on Deal activity, scoring, and AI analysis.

---

## Best Practices for Using Smart Nudges

- Review recommendations regularly.
- Prioritize High-priority nudges.
- Act on recommendations promptly.
- Combine AI recommendations with sales judgment and customer context.
- Monitor score changes after completing recommended actions.
