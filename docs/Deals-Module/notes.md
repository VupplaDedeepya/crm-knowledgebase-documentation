# Notes Management

The **Notes** feature allows users to capture important information, discussions, observations, and follow-up details related to a Deal.

Notes help sales teams maintain a complete history of customer interactions, making it easier for team members to understand the context of an opportunity and collaborate effectively.

---

## Accessing Notes

1. Navigate to the **Deals** module.
2. Open the required Deal record.
3. Go to the **Notes** tab under the **Activities** section.
4. Click **Add Note**.

This opens the **Note Editor**.

![Create Note](/img/Deals/DealCreateNote.png)

---

## Creating a Note

After opening the Note Editor:

1. Enter the required note content.
2. Format the text if required.
3. Click **Submit**.

The note is saved and displayed in the Deal timeline.

---

## Note Editor Features

The Note Editor provides rich text editing capabilities, allowing users to:

- Enter formatted text.
- Create bullet and numbered lists.
- Insert hyperlinks.
- Add images.
- Create tables.
- Highlight important information.

---

## Managing Notes

Users can perform the following actions on existing notes:

- View notes.
- Edit notes.
- Delete notes.
- Search notes.
- Pin important notes.

---

## Viewing Notes

All notes are displayed in chronological order under the **Notes** section of the Deal.

Each note displays:

- Author
- Date and Time
- Note Content

This provides complete visibility into discussions and updates throughout the Deal lifecycle.

---

## Best Practices

- Record customer discussions immediately after meetings or calls.
- Keep notes clear and concise.
- Include important decisions and action items.
- Update notes whenever new information becomes available.
- Use notes to maintain a complete history of customer interactions.
