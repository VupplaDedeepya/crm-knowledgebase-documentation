# Linked Deals

The **Linked Deals** tab provides a consolidated view of all sales opportunities associated with a Contact. This enables users to understand the revenue opportunities connected to a specific individual and track the progress of ongoing and historical Deals without leaving the Contact record.

---

## Overview

A Contact can be associated with multiple Deals throughout the customer lifecycle. The **Linked Deals** tab serves as a central location for viewing these relationships and monitoring Deal activity.

The tab displays all Deals linked to the Contact, regardless of their current status, allowing users to maintain complete visibility into the Contact's sales journey.

![Linked Deals](/img/Contacts/LinkedDeals.png)

---

## Deal Relationship Management

Every Deal in Sales Astra CRM must be associated with a valid Contact. This relationship ensures that revenue opportunities remain connected to the individuals involved in the buying process.

A single Contact can be linked to multiple Deals, including:

- Active Deals
- Won Deals
- Lost Deals
- Historical Opportunities

This allows users to track the complete engagement history of a Contact across multiple sales cycles.

---

## Viewing Linked Deals

To view linked Deals:

1. Navigate to the **Contacts** module.
2. Open the required Contact record.
3. Select the **Linked Deals** tab.

The system displays all Deals associated with the Contact.

---

## Deal Summary

At the top of the page, Sales Astra CRM displays key Deal metrics for the Contact.

The summary includes:

- **Total Linked Deals** – Total number of Deals associated with the Contact.
- **Open Deals** – Number of active Deals currently in progress.
- **Won Deals** – Number of successfully closed Deals.
- **Total Deal Value** – Combined value of all linked Deals.

These metrics provide users with a quick overview of the Contact's overall sales engagement.

---

## Viewing Deal Cards

Each linked Deal is displayed as an individual Deal card containing:

- Deal Name
- Deal Status
- Deal Value
- Created Date

This allows users to quickly review the Contact's associated opportunities and their current status.


---

## Opening a Deal

Each Deal card includes a **View Deal** option.

Clicking **View Deal** opens the corresponding **Deal Detail** page, where users can:

- View complete Deal information.
- Track Deal progress.
- Review activities.
- Manage the opportunity throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Benefits

The **Linked Deals** tab helps users:

- View all Deals associated with a Contact.
- Monitor active and completed opportunities.
- Understand the Contact's revenue contribution.
- Access Deal records directly from the Contact profile.
- Maintain visibility into the Contact's sales history.
