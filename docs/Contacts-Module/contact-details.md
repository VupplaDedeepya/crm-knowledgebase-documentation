# Contact Details

The **Contact Detail** page provides a centralized view of all information, interactions, and relationships associated with a Contact. It enables users to manage contact information, track engagement history, create follow-up activities, and monitor related business records such as Leads and Deals from a single location.

The page is divided into multiple sections and tabs that help users maintain a complete view of customer interactions and ongoing opportunities.

---

## Contact Overview

The left panel displays the Contact's profile information and key details, including:

- Contact Name
- Email Address
- Contact Owner
- Source
- Engagement Status
- Phone Numbers
- Company Information
- Communication Details
- Additional Information

This information is displayed according to the fields configured by the administrator for the organization.

## Contact Actions

The **Contact Detail** page provides quick-action buttons that allow users to perform common CRM activities without leaving the Contact record.

Available actions include:

- Edit Contact
- Create Note
- Create Task
- Create Deal
- Log Activity

These actions can also be performed from their respective tabs where applicable.


---

## Edit Contact

Use the **Edit Contact** option to update existing Contact information.

### Steps

1. Open the **Contact Detail** page.
2. Click the **Edit** icon.
3. Modify the required fields.
4. Click **Save**.

Any changes made are reflected immediately in the Contact record.

---

## Activity Timeline

Logged activities automatically appear in the Contact's **Activity Timeline**, ensuring a complete record of interactions is maintained.

The timeline provides a chronological view of all activities associated with the Contact, allowing users to quickly review customer interactions and follow-up history.

Activities that appear in the timeline may include:

- Notes
- Tasks
- Meetings
- Emails
- Logged Activities
- Deal Creation
- Other CRM events

This centralized timeline helps sales teams maintain complete visibility into customer engagement throughout the relationship.


---

## Contact Detail Tabs

The **Contact Detail** page also includes dedicated tabs for managing related information.

### Activities

Displays the chronological history of interactions, updates, tasks, notes, and other CRM activities associated with the Contact.

---

### Notes

Displays all notes created for the Contact and allows users to create and manage additional notes.

---

### Tasks

Displays all tasks associated with the Contact and allows users to create and manage follow-up activities.

---

### Linked Deals

Displays all Deals associated with the Contact, allowing users to monitor active opportunities, review deal progress, and access Deal records directly from the Contact profile.

---

### Linked Leads

Displays all Leads associated with the Contact, helping users understand the Contact's acquisition journey, review Lead history, and access Lead records directly from the Contact profile.
