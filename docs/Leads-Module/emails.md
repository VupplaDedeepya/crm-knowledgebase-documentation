# Sending Emails from Lead Activities

The **Email** feature allows CRM users to compose, send, and track emails directly from the CRM system without switching to external email applications.

All email communication is automatically recorded within the lead activity timeline, helping users maintain complete communication history and improve lead engagement tracking.

---

## Accessing the Email Section

### Steps to Open Email Composer

- Navigate to the **Leads** Module.
- Open the required lead record.
- Go to the **Email** tab under Activities.
- Click **Compose Email**.

This opens the **Email Composer** window.

![Compose Email](/img/Leads/ComposeEmail.png)

---

## Email Templates

Users can insert pre-saved email templates into the email body.

Templates help:

- Save time
- Maintain communication consistency
- Improve productivity

---

## Insert Options

The Email Composer supports insertion of:

- Email templates
- CRM documents
- Meeting links
- Quotes

directly into the email body.

---

## Meeting Links

Users can insert meeting links from connected calendar providers such as:

- Google Meet
- Microsoft Teams
- Outlook Meet

This helps schedule discussions directly through email communication.

---

## Send Options

The Email module supports multiple sending options.

| Option | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Send Now | Sends email immediately |
| Schedule Send | Sends email at selected date and time |
| Save Draft | Saves email for later editing |

---

## Schedule Send

Users can schedule emails for future delivery.

Scheduled emails appear under **Scheduled Activities** until they are sent.

---

## Save Draft

Users can save incomplete emails as drafts and continue editing later.

Draft emails display status as **Draft**.

---

## Email Status Tracking

The system tracks email statuses such as:

- Draft
- Sent
- Failed

This helps users monitor communication activity.

---

## Email Activity Logging

All sent emails are automatically logged in the **Lead Activity Timeline**.

The logged activity includes:

- Timestamp
- Subject
- Email preview snippet
- Sender information

---

## Associations

Email activities are automatically associated with:

- Lead records
- Related contacts
- Related deals

This helps maintain centralized communication history.
