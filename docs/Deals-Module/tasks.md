# Deal Activities Management

The **Deals** module supports comprehensive activity management to help sales teams track all customer interactions and next steps directly within the Deal lifecycle.

Activities help maintain a centralized history of communication, collaboration, follow-ups, and engagement related to a Deal.

---

## Purpose of Deal Activities

Deal Activities help organizations:

- Track all customer touchpoints.
- Maintain communication history.
- Improve collaboration.
- Monitor follow-ups.
- Increase visibility into Deal progress.
- Improve customer engagement management.

---

## Deal Activities Timeline

All activities are displayed inside the **Deal Details** page under the **Activities** section.

Activities appear in a chronological timeline for easy tracking and visibility.

---

## Activity Filters

Users can filter activities by:

- Meetings
- Emails
- Tasks
- Notes

This helps users quickly locate specific interactions.

---

## Tasks Management

Tasks help sales teams manage action items and follow-ups related to Deals.

Users can:

- Create Tasks
- Assign Tasks
- Set reminders
- Track completion status

---

### Step 1: Open the Deal Record

- Navigate to the **Deals** Module.
- Open the required Deal record.
- Go to the **Tasks** tab inside the **Activities** section.

---

### Step 2: Create a New Task

- Click **Create Task**.

This opens the **Task Creation** form.

![Create Task](/img/deals/DealCreateTask.png)

---

## Task Statuses

Common statuses include:

- Open
- In Progress
- Completed
- Overdue

---

## Task Validations

The following validations are applied while creating a task:

- Title is required.
- Title length must be between **3–100 characters**.
- Due date must be **today or a future date**.
- Priority defaults to **Medium** if not selected.

---

## Task Notifications

The CRM sends the following notifications:

- Due date reminders.
- Assignment notifications.
- Follow-up alerts.

Notifications are delivered through in-app notifications or email.

---

### Step 3: Save the Task

After entering the required details:

- Click the **Save** button.

The task will be created and linked to the selected Deal.
