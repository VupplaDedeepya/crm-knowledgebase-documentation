# Contacts Overview

The **Contacts** module in Sales Astra CRM serves as the central repository for managing customer and stakeholder information throughout the customer lifecycle. It enables organizations to maintain accurate customer records, build meaningful relationships, and connect Contacts with other CRM records such as Leads, Deals, Organizations, Activities, and Conversations.

A Contact represents an individual associated with your business, such as a customer, prospect, decision-maker, or stakeholder. By maintaining a centralized contact database, organizations can improve collaboration, eliminate duplicate records, and gain a complete view of customer interactions across the sales process.

The Contacts module provides comprehensive tools for creating, organizing, managing, and tracking customer information while ensuring that every interaction and business opportunity is associated with the appropriate Contact.

---

## What You Can Do with the Contacts Module

The Contacts module provides a comprehensive set of features to help users efficiently manage customer information, including:

- Create and manage Contact records.
- Associate Contacts with Organizations.
- Link Contacts to Leads and Deals.
- Store communication and address information.
- Perform Bulk Actions on multiple Contacts.
- Import and Export Contact records.
- Create personalized Custom Views.
- Search and filter Contact records.
- Create and manage Notes and Tasks.
- Log customer interactions and activities.
- View Linked Deals and Linked Leads.
- Track customer engagement throughout the sales lifecycle.

## Contact Management Capabilities

The Contacts module enables users to manage customer information by providing tools for:

- Maintaining customer profiles.

- Organizing Contacts using Tags and custom fields.

- Preventing duplicate Contact records.

## Contact Activities

Each Contact maintains a centralized activity history that provides complete visibility into customer interactions.

Users can:

- Create Notes
- Create Tasks
- Log Activities
- View Activity Timeline
- Create Deals
- Monitor customer engagement history

This centralized activity history helps teams collaborate effectively and maintain complete customer context throughout the sales process.

---

## Relationship Management

The Contacts module helps maintain relationships between various CRM records.

Users can:

- Associate Contacts with Organizations.
- View all Deals linked to a Contact.
- Track Leads associated with a Contact.
- Access related records directly from the Contact Detail page.

This interconnected structure provides a complete view of every customer's journey, from initial Lead generation to active sales opportunities and ongoing customer relationships.
