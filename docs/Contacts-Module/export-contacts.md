# Export Contacts

The **Export Contacts** feature allows users to download Contact data from Sales Astra CRM for reporting, analysis, auditing, backup, or migration purposes.

Users can export all Contacts, filtered Contacts, or selected Contact records based on their requirements.

---

## Accessing the Export Feature

1. Navigate to the **Contacts** module.
2. Click the **Export** button.
3. Select the Contacts to export, if applicable.
4. Generate and download the export file.

---

## Export Options

Users can export:

### All Contacts

Exports all Contact records available in the selected view.

---

### Filtered Contacts

Exports only the Contact records that match the currently applied filters or Custom View.

---

### Selected Contacts

Exports only the Contact records selected using the checkboxes in the Contacts list.

---

## Export Benefits

The **Export Contacts** feature helps users:

- Create offline backups of Contact records.
- Share Contact data with authorized stakeholders.
- Generate reports and perform analysis.
- Support migration to external systems.
- Maintain records for auditing and compliance purposes.

---

## Common Use Cases

### Reporting and Analysis

Export Contact data to generate business reports, customer insights, and performance analysis.

---

### Data Backup

Maintain an offline copy of Contact records for recovery and archival purposes.

---

### Data Sharing

Share Contact information securely with authorized team members or external stakeholders.

---

### Data Migration

Export Contact records for migration to another CRM or external business application.

---

## Best Practices

- Apply filters before exporting to include only the required Contact records.
- Verify the exported data before sharing it externally.
- Ensure compliance with your organization's data privacy and security policies.
- Export only the information necessary for the intended business purpose.
