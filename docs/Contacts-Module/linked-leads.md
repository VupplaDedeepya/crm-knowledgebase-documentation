# Linked Leads

The **Linked Leads** tab provides visibility into all Leads that have been associated with a Contact throughout its lifecycle. This allows users to understand how a Contact entered the CRM and review historical Lead activity, including converted Leads.

---

## Overview

Contacts often originate from Leads captured through various channels such as website inquiries, marketing campaigns, referrals, or manual entry.

When a Lead is converted, the relationship between the Lead and the Contact is preserved, allowing users to maintain historical tracking and visibility into the Contact's journey.

The **Linked Leads** tab serves as a historical record of these relationships.

![Linked Leads](/img/Contacts/LinkedLeads.png)

---

## Viewing Linked Leads

To view linked Leads:

1. Navigate to the **Contacts** module.
2. Open the required Contact record.
3. Select the **Linked Leads** tab.

The system displays all Leads associated with the Contact.

---

## Lead Information

Each linked Lead is displayed as an individual Lead card containing key information such as:

- Lead Name
- Lead Status
- Lead Source
- Created Date

This information helps users quickly understand how the Contact was acquired and the status of associated Lead records.


---

## Opening a Lead

Each Lead card includes a **View Lead** option.

Clicking **View Lead** opens the corresponding **Lead Detail** page, where users can:

- View complete Lead information.
- Review Lead activities.
- View communication history.
- Track Lead qualification and conversion progress.

---

## Relationship Management

The relationship between a Contact and its associated Leads is maintained automatically by the CRM.

This helps users:

- Trace the origin of a Contact.
- Maintain Lead conversion history.
- Understand the customer acquisition journey.
- Preserve historical Lead information even after conversion.

---

## Benefits

The **Linked Leads** tab helps users:

- Understand the origin of a Contact.
- Review associated Lead records.
- Track Lead qualification and conversion history.
- Access Lead details directly from the Contact profile.
- Maintain visibility into the Contact's acquisition journey.
