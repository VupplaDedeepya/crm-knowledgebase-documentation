# Proposals Management

The **Proposals** functionality in the Deals Module helps sales teams create, upload, manage, send, and track customer-facing proposal documents directly within a Deal.

Proposals help organizations maintain professional communication, present business solutions effectively, and centralize proposal management throughout the Deal lifecycle.

---

## What is a Proposal?

A **Proposal** is a formal business document shared with customers that explains:

- Customer requirements
- Proposed solution
- Scope of work
- Deliverables
- Pricing overview
- Terms and conditions

Proposals are usually shared during the **Qualification**, **Discussion**, or **Negotiation** stages of a Deal.

---

## Importance of Proposals

Proposals help organizations:

- Present solutions professionally.
- Maintain standardized documentation.
- Improve customer understanding.
- Track proposal discussions.
- Improve sales communication.
- Increase Deal conversion efficiency.

---

## Accessing Proposals

Proposals can be accessed from the **Deal Details** page under the **Proposals** tab.


---

## Creating a Proposal

### Step 1: Open Deal Record

- Navigate to the **Deals** module.
- Open the required Deal.
- Go to the **Proposals** tab.

---

### Step 2: Click Add

- Click the **Add** button.

This opens the **Proposal Creation Options** window.

![Proposal Options](/img/Deals/CreateProposal.png)

## Proposal Creation Options

The CRM provides multiple ways to create or manage proposals.

![upload-proposal](/img/deals/ProposalOptions.png)

---

## Option 1: Upload Proposal

This option allows users to upload an already prepared proposal document.

### Steps

1. Click **Upload Proposal**.
2. Upload the proposal document.
3. Select the proposal status.
4. Add recipient details if required.
5. Select the communication channel.
6. Add notes if needed.
7. Click **Upload**.

![upload-proposal](/img/deals/UploadProposal.png)

---

### Upload Proposal Features

Users can:

- Upload PDF, DOC, or DOCX files.
- Associate proposals with Deals.
- Add recipients.
- Add internal notes.
- Track uploaded proposals.

---

### Supported File Formats

The CRM supports:

- PDF
- DOC
- DOCX

> **Note**
>
> The maximum file size may vary depending on your organization's configuration.

---

### Proposal Status

Users can assign statuses such as:

- Draft
- Pending Approval
- Sent
- Approved
- Rejected

---

## Option 2: Upload & Send via CXAstra

This option allows users to upload and directly send proposals using CRM communication channels.

### Features

- Centralized proposal sharing.
- Email tracking.
- Customer engagement tracking.
- Activity timeline logging.

![Send-via-CXAstra](/img/deals/SendViaCXAstra.png)

---

## Option 3: Create from Template

This option allows users to create proposals directly inside the CRM using predefined templates.

### Steps

1. Click **Create from Template**.
2. Select the required proposal template.
3. Click **Continue**.
4. Enter the proposal details.
5. Save the proposal.

![Create-From-Template](/img/deals/CreateFromTemplate.png)

---

### Benefits of Proposal Templates

Proposal templates help organizations:

- Maintain consistent formatting.
- Reduce manual work.
- Improve branding consistency.
- Speed up proposal creation.

---

## Proposal Lifecycle

Proposals generally move through the following stages:

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Draft | Proposal created but not finalized. |
| Pending Approval | Waiting for approval. |
| Approved | Approved for customer sharing. |
| Sent | Shared with the customer. |
| Accepted | Customer accepted the proposal. |
| Rejected | Customer rejected the proposal. |

---

## Activity Timeline Integration

All proposal activities are automatically recorded in the **Deal Activity Timeline**.

This includes:

- Proposal uploads.
- Status changes.
- Sharing activities.
- Approval updates.
- Customer interactions.

This provides complete visibility into the proposal lifecycle directly from the Deal record.

---

## AI and Smart Insights

Sales Astra CRM analyzes proposal activities to provide valuable insights, including:

- Deal Scoring.
- Engagement Insights.
- Smart Nudges.
- Follow-up Recommendations.

Customer interactions with proposals help the CRM determine engagement levels and identify opportunities that may require immediate attention.

---

## Best Practices

- Use proposal templates to maintain consistency.
- Review proposal content before sharing it with customers.
- Keep proposal statuses updated throughout the sales process.
- Attach supporting documents when necessary.
- Monitor customer engagement and follow up promptly after proposals are shared.
- Regularly review proposal activities from the Deal Activity Timeline.
