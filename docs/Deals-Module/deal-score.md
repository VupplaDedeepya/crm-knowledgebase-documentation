# Deal Scoring

The **Deal Score** provides a numerical indication of the overall health and quality of a Deal. It helps sales teams prioritize opportunities and focus on Deals that are more likely to close successfully.

The score is calculated using a combination of:

- Business Rules configured by administrators (Rule-Based Scoring).
- AI Analysis based on buyer behavior, engagement patterns, Deal activity, and other predictive signals.

Administrators can configure the contribution of each scoring method by defining the percentage ratio between **Business Rule Scoring** and **AI Scoring** in the Deal Scoring settings.

---

### Example

#### Scoring Configuration

- Business Rules: **60%**
- AI Analysis: **40%**

In this configuration:

- 60% of the final score is derived from predefined Business Rules.
- 40% of the final score is derived from AI-driven analysis.

The system combines both components to generate a single **Deal Score** that reflects both organizational priorities and real-world buyer behavior.

---

## Benefits of Deal Scoring

Deal Scoring helps organizations:

- Prioritize high-potential opportunities.
- Identify Deals requiring attention.
- Provide objective Deal health measurement.
- Combine business expertise with AI-driven insights.

---

## Deal Score Trend

The **Deal Score Trend** section provides a visual representation of how a Deal's score changes over time. It helps users understand whether the overall health of a Deal is improving, declining, or remaining stable throughout the sales cycle.

Since the Deal Score is calculated using a combination of **Business Rule Scoring** and **AI Scoring**, the Score Trend reflects the combined impact of both scoring methods.

---

## Purpose of the Score Trend

The Score Trend enables users to:

- Monitor Deal health over time.
- Track the effectiveness of sales activities.
- Identify positive or negative engagement patterns.
- Detect potential Deal risks early.
- Measure the impact of buyer interactions and Deal progression.
- Understand how changes in scoring factors affect the overall Deal Score.

---

## Understanding the Score Trend Chart

The chart displays historical changes in the Deal Score across a selected period.

![Deal-Score-Trend](/img/deals/DealScoreTrend.png)

### A Rising Trend Generally Indicates

- Increased buyer engagement.
- Positive Deal progression.
- Completion of important sales activities.
- Improved AI confidence in Deal success.
- Achievement of scoring criteria defined by Business Rules.

---

### A Declining Trend May Indicate

- Reduced customer engagement.
- Missed follow-ups or activities.
- Stalled Deal progression.
- Negative engagement signals identified by AI.
- Changes in Rule-Based Scoring conditions.

---

### A Stable Trend May Indicate

- Consistent engagement.
- No significant changes in Deal activity.
- Steady progression through the sales process.

---

## Factors That Influence Score Changes

Score fluctuations can occur due to updates in either **Business Rule Scoring** or **AI Scoring**.

---

## Business Rule Factors

Examples include:

- Deal moving to a new stage.
- Changes to Deal Value.
- Completion of required activities.
- Addition or removal of Tags.
- Updates to important Deal fields.
- Changes to administrator-defined scoring rules.

---

## AI Factors

Examples include:

- Buyer responsiveness.
- Email and meeting engagement.
- Activity frequency.
- Communication patterns.
- Historical conversion indicators.
- Changes in Deal momentum and risk signals.

Because the final Deal Score combines both scoring methods, changes from either source may affect the trend.

---

## Viewing Historical Trends

Users can analyze score performance across different time periods.

Available options may include:

- Weekly (W)
- Monthly (M)
- Yearly (Y)
- All Time (All)

These views help users identify short-term score fluctuations as well as long-term Deal performance patterns.

---

## Using Score Trends Effectively

Sales teams can use the Score Trend to:

- Identify Deals gaining momentum.
- Detect opportunities requiring immediate attention.
- Validate the effectiveness of sales activities.
- Prioritize high-potential opportunities.
- Support forecasting and pipeline reviews.
- Monitor the impact of Smart Nudge recommendations and follow-up actions.

---

## Best Practices

- Review Score Trends regularly during pipeline reviews.
- Investigate significant score increases or decreases.
- Act promptly when a Deal's score begins to decline.
- Use Score Trends together with Smart Nudges for better decision-making.
- Consider both Business Rule factors and AI insights when evaluating score changes.
