# Activities

The **Activities** module provides a centralized workspace for managing all CRM-related activities across **Leads, Deals, Contacts, and Organizations**. It consolidates tasks and AI-generated smart nudges into a single location, enabling users to track, prioritize, and complete activities efficiently without navigating between individual records.

Activities created from this module are automatically linked to their associated CRM records and are reflected throughout the system, ensuring consistent activity tracking and visibility.


---

## Activities Workspace

The **Activities** page displays all assigned activities in a unified list view and allows users to:

- View all tasks and smart nudges in one place.
- Monitor upcoming and overdue activities.
- Create new tasks.
- Search and filter activities.
- Sort activities by **Due Date**, **Priority**, and **Recent**.
- Access activities associated with Leads, Deals, Contacts, or Organizations.
- Mark important activities for quick access.

---

## Activity Tabs

The Activities workspace is divided into three tabs.

### All Activities

Displays all activities assigned to the user, including both manually created tasks and AI-generated smart nudges.

This view provides a complete overview of pending work and recommended actions across the CRM.

---

### Tasks

Displays only manually created tasks.

Use this tab when you want to focus exclusively on planned follow-ups, calls, meetings, reminders, or other user-created activities.

---

### Smart Nudges

Displays AI-generated recommendations that help users take timely actions on CRM records.

Examples include:

- Lead has not been contacted recently.
- Deal value has increased.
- Follow-up is overdue.
- Customer engagement has declined.

Smart Nudges help ensure that important opportunities and actions are not overlooked.


---

## Filtering Activities

Activities can be filtered to quickly locate relevant records.

Available filters include:

### Status Filter

Filter activities based on their current status.

Examples may include:

- Pending
- In Progress
- Completed
- Overdue


---

Filter activities based on due dates or activity dates.

## Priority Filter

Filter activities according to their assigned priority level.

- Low
- Medium
- High

Multiple filters can be applied simultaneously to narrow results.

## Searching Activities

The Activities module includes a search feature that allows users to quickly locate activities.

Users can search using:

- Activity title
- Associated record name
- Keywords within the activity

Search results update dynamically as text is entered.


---

## Creating a Task

Tasks can be created directly from the Activities module.

### Steps

1. Click **New Task**.
2. Complete the task details.
3. Click **Submit**.

The task is immediately added to:

- The Activities list.
- The associated Lead, Deal, Contact, or Organization record.
