# Meetings Management

The **Meetings** feature enables users to schedule, manage, and track meetings related to Deals directly within the CRM. It helps sales teams collaborate with customers, record important discussions, and maintain a complete history of interactions throughout the sales process.

Meetings can also be synchronized with supported calendar providers, ensuring schedules remain up to date across integrated platforms.

---

## Accessing Meetings

1. Navigate to the **Deals** module.
2. Open the required Deal record.
3. Go to the **Meetings** tab under the **Activities** section.
4. Click **Add**.

This opens the **Meeting Scheduling** form.

![Create Meeting](/img/deals/DealCreateMeeting.png)

---

## Scheduling a Meeting

Users can schedule meetings by providing the required information.

### Meeting Details

| Field | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Event Title | Specifies the title of the meeting. |
| From | Displays the meeting organizer. |
| Attendees | Select internal users or external attendees. |
| Start Date & Time | Specifies when the meeting begins. |
| End Date & Time | Specifies when the meeting ends. |
| Location | Select the meeting location or virtual meeting provider. |
| Description | Add the meeting agenda or discussion points. |

---

## Meeting Location

Users can schedule meetings using supported meeting platforms such as:

- Google Meet
- Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft Outlook
- In Person

If an online meeting provider is selected, the meeting link is automatically generated and shared with attendees.

---

## Attendees

The Deal's primary contact is automatically added as an attendee.

Users can also:

- Add internal CRM users.
- Invite external participants using their email addresses.

---

## Description

The Description field allows users to record:

- Meeting agenda
- Discussion topics
- Preparation notes
- Follow-up items

Providing detailed descriptions helps participants prepare before the meeting.

---

## Saving the Meeting

After entering the required details:

1. Review the meeting information.
2. Click **Save**.

The meeting is:

- Added to the Deal Activity Timeline.
- Synchronized with the connected calendar.
- Shared with all invited attendees.


---

## Rescheduling Meetings

Users can modify scheduled meetings whenever required.

The following details can be updated:

- Date
- Time
- Attendees
- Meeting Location
- Description

Changes are automatically synchronized with the connected calendar.

---

# Logging Meetings

The **Log Meeting** feature allows users to manually record meetings that have already taken place.

Logging meetings helps maintain an accurate interaction history and supports future reporting and collaboration.

![Log-meeting](/img/deals/DealLogMeeting.png)

---

## Log Meeting Details

Users can record information such as:

| Field | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Meeting Title | Title of the meeting |
| Meeting Type | Virtual, In-Person, or Phone |
| Date & Time | Date and time when the meeting occurred |
| Participants | Internal and external attendees |
| Summary | Summary of the discussion |
| Outcome | Result of the meeting |
| Follow-up Task | Create a follow-up task if required |
| Attachments | Upload supporting documents |

---

## Meeting Outcomes

Users can record outcomes such as:

- Successful
- Follow-up Required
- Rescheduled
- No Response
- No Show
- Cancelled

Recording outcomes helps track customer engagement and determine the next course of action.

---

## Attachments

Supporting documents can be uploaded while logging a meeting.

Examples include:

- Presentation files
- Proposal documents
- Meeting notes
- Images
- Other supporting files


---

## Saving a Logged Meeting

After entering all required information:

1. Review the meeting details.
2. Click **Submit**.

The meeting is successfully logged and added to the Deal Activity Timeline.
