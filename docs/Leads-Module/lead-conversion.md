# Lead to Deal Conversion

The CRM system supports **Lead to Deal Conversion**, allowing qualified leads to be converted into active business opportunities.

This functionality helps sales teams move potential customers from the lead management stage into the sales pipeline for further opportunity tracking and revenue management.

---

## What is Lead to Deal Conversion?

Lead to Deal Conversion is the process of converting a qualified lead into a deal once the lead shows strong business potential or buying intent.

After conversion:

- The lead becomes an active sales opportunity.
- A deal record is created.
- The opportunity enters the sales pipeline.
- Sales teams can start negotiation and deal management activities.

---

## When Does Lead Conversion Happen?

A lead can be converted into a deal in two ways:

- Automatic Conversion: Triggered when lead status changes to **Qualified**.
- Manual Conversion: Triggered using the **Convert to Deal** button.

---

## Automatic Lead to Deal Conversion

When a lead status is updated to **Qualified**, the CRM automatically opens the Deal Creation form.

This helps sales users immediately create a business opportunity without manually navigating to the Deals Module.

---

### Step 1: Open Lead Record

- Navigate to the **Leads** Module.
- Open the required lead record.

---

### Step 2: Change Lead Status

- Update the lead status to **Qualified**.

![Qualify-Lead](/img/leads/QualifyLead.png)

---

### Step 3: Deal Creation Form Appears

Once the lead is marked as **Qualified**:

- The Deal Creation popup automatically appears.
- Lead information is pre-filled into the deal form.

![Lead Conversion](/img/Leads/LeadConversion.png)

---

## Manual Lead to Deal Conversion

Users can also manually convert a lead into a deal from the **Lead Details** page.

---

## Manual Conversion Process

### Step 1: Open Lead Details Page

- Navigate to the **Leads** Module.
- Open the required lead record.

---

### Step 2: Click Convert to Deal

- Click the Convert to Deal button.
- A confirmation pop-up appears asking whether you want to update the lead status to Qualified.

![Qualify-Lead](/img/leads/QualifyLead.png)

- Click Yes to proceed. The Deal Creation form opens, allowing you to create a new deal from the qualified lead.


---

## Pre-Filled Information

The system may automatically populate fields such as:

| Field | Auto Filled from Lead |
|--------|-----------------------|
| Deal Title | Lead Name |
| Contact | Lead Contact |
| Account | Associated Organization |
| Deal Owner | Current Lead Owner |
| Pipeline | Default Sales Pipeline |
| Stage | Qualification |
| Status | Open |

---

## Deal Fields During Conversion

Users can update or enter additional information such as:

- Buyer Type
- Deal Value
- Close Date
- Tags
- Currency
- Pipeline Stage

before creating the deal.


---

## Relationship Between Lead and Deal

After conversion:

- The deal remains linked to the original lead.
- Activity history is preserved.
- Customer interactions remain traceable.

This helps maintain a complete sales history throughout the lifecycle.

---

## Benefits of Lead to Deal Conversion

Lead conversion helps organizations:

- Move qualified leads into the sales pipeline quickly.
- Reduce manual data entry.
- Improve sales workflow efficiency.
- Maintain relationship continuity.
- Improve opportunity tracking.
- Increase conversion visibility.
