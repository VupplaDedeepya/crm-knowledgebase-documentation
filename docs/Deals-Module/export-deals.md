# Export Deals

The **Export Deals** feature allows users to download Deal records from the CRM for reporting, analysis, data sharing, auditing, and backup purposes.

Users can export all Deals, filtered Deals, or selected Deal records based on their requirements.

---

## Accessing Deal Export

1. Navigate to the **Deals** module.
2. Apply filters or select a Custom View if required.
3. Click **Export**.
4. Configure export options, if available.
5. Click **Export** to generate the file.
6. Download the exported file.

![Export Deals](/img/deals/ExportDeals.png)

---

## Export Options

Users can export:

### All Deals

Exports all Deal records available in the selected view.

---

### Filtered Deals

Exports only Deals matching the applied filters.

---

### Selected Deals

Exports only the Deal records selected using the checkboxes in the Deal list.

---

## Common Use Cases

### Sales Reporting

Generate reports for management reviews and performance analysis.

---

### Pipeline Analysis

Analyze Deal progression, pipeline health, and forecasting data.

---

### Data Sharing

Share Deal information with internal teams or stakeholders.

---

### Data Backup

Maintain an offline copy of Deal records for auditing and recovery purposes.

---

### Business Intelligence

Use exported data in spreadsheets, reporting tools, or analytics platforms.

---

## Best Practices

- Apply filters before exporting to obtain relevant records.
- Verify selected columns before generating exports.
- Review exported data for accuracy.
- Follow organizational data privacy and security guidelines when sharing exported files.
- Export only the information required for the intended purpose.
