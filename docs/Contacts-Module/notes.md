# Notes

The **Notes** feature allows users to capture important information, meeting summaries, customer discussions, observations, and follow-up details related to a Contact.

Notes help maintain a complete history of customer interactions, making it easier for team members to understand the context of conversations and collaborate effectively throughout the customer relationship.

---

## Accessing Notes

1. Navigate to the **Contacts** module.
2. Open the required Contact record.
3. Go to the **Notes** tab under the **Activities** section.
4. Click **Create Note**.

This opens the **Note Editor**.

![Create Note](/img/Contacts/ContactsCreateNote.png)

---

## Creating a Note

After opening the Note Editor:

1. Enter the note content using the rich text editor.
2. Format the content as required.
3. Optionally:
   - Create a follow-up reminder.
   - Create a To-Do item associated with the note.
4. Click **Submit**.

The note is automatically associated with the Contact and becomes visible in both the **Activity Timeline** and **Notes** tab.

---

## Note Features

The Notes feature provides the following capabilities:

- Rich text formatting support.
- Follow-up reminders.
- To-Do creation from notes.
- Automatic association with the Contact record.
- Visibility within the Contact Timeline and Notes tab.

---

## Managing Notes

Users can perform the following actions on existing notes:

- View notes.
- Edit notes.
- Delete notes.
- Search notes.
- Pin important notes.

---

## Viewing Notes

All notes are displayed in chronological order under the **Notes** tab.

Each note displays:

- Author
- Date and Time
- Note Content

This provides complete visibility into discussions and updates throughout the customer lifecycle.


---

## Benefits

The Notes feature helps users:

- Maintain conversation history.
- Record meeting outcomes.
- Capture customer requirements.
- Preserve important contextual information.
- Improve collaboration among team members.
- Maintain a complete history of customer interactions.

---

## Best Practices

- Record customer discussions immediately after meetings or calls.
- Keep notes clear and concise.
- Include important decisions and action items.
- Update notes whenever new information becomes available.
- Use notes to maintain a complete history of customer interactions.
