# Facebook Messenger & Instagram Integration

## Overview

The Facebook and Instagram channels allow CRM to receive and manage customer conversations from Facebook Messenger and Instagram Direct Messages within the Pulse Inbox.

Once connected, incoming messages from both channels can be handled directly from the CRM without switching between Meta applications.

---

# Prerequisites

Before connecting the channels, ensure that:

- You have a Facebook account.
- You have administrator access to the Facebook Page.
- Your Facebook Page has Messenger enabled.
- Your Instagram account is a Professional (Business or Creator) account.
- The Instagram account is linked to the Facebook Page.
- You have permission to manage both the Facebook Page and the Instagram account.
- The Facebook Page and the Instagram Professional account must not be owned by another Business Portfolio.

---

# Steps to Connect Facebook Messenger & Instagram

## Step 1: Navigate to the Channels Page

Go to:

**Pulse Settings → Channels**

The Channels page displays all the available communication channels.

![Channels Page](/img/facebook/facebook-step1.png)

---

## Step 2: Connect Facebook

Locate the **Facebook** channel and click **Connect**.

![Connect Facebook](/img/facebook/facebook-step2.png)

:::note

Connecting Facebook also connects the linked Instagram account automatically.

A separate Instagram connection is not required.

:::

---

## Step 3: Sign in to Facebook

A Meta authorization window appears.

Sign in using the Facebook account that manages your Facebook Page and the linked Instagram Professional account.

![Facebook Login](/img/facebook/facebook-step3.png)

:::note

A single Facebook account can be used to connect multiple Meta channels, including:

- WhatsApp Business
- Facebook Messenger
- Instagram

Make sure the Facebook account you sign in with has administrator access to the Business Portfolio and the required business assets.

:::

---

## Step 4: Continue as Your Facebook Account

After signing in, Meta displays a confirmation screen showing the Facebook account that will be used for the authorization.

Review the displayed account information.

Click **Continue as _Your Facebook Account_** to proceed.

![Continue as Facebook User](/img/facebook/facebook-step4.png)

---

## Step 5: Select the Facebook Page

Meta displays the Facebook Pages that you manage.

You can choose one of the following options:

- **Opt in to all current and future Pages** – Grants the CRM access to all your existing Pages and any Pages you create in the future.

![Select Facebook Page](/img/facebook/facebook-step5a.png)

- **Opt in to current Pages only** – Allows you to manually select the Facebook Page(s) you want to authorize.

![Select Facebook Page](/img/facebook/facebook-step5b.png)

If you choose **Opt in to current Pages only**:

1. Select the checkbox next to the Facebook Page you want to connect.
2. Verify that the correct Facebook Page is selected.
3. Click **Continue**.



:::note

Only the selected Facebook Page(s) will be accessible to the CRM.

:::

---
---

## Step 6: Select the Business Account

Meta now asks you to choose which Business Account(s) the CRM can access.

You can choose one of the following options:

- **Opt in to all current and future Businesses** – Grants the CRM access to all your existing Business Accounts and any Business Accounts you create in the future.

![Select Business Account](/img/facebook/facebook-step6a.png)

- **Opt in to current Businesses only** – Allows you to manually select the Business Account(s) you want to authorize.

If you select **Opt in to current Businesses only**:

1. Select the checkbox next to the Business Account you want to connect.
2. Verify that the correct Business Account is selected.
3. Click **Continue**.

:::note

Only the selected Business Account(s) will be accessible to the CRM.

:::

![Select Business Account](/img/facebook/facebook-step6b.png)

---

## Step 7: Select the Instagram Account

Meta displays the Instagram account(s) associated with your Facebook Page.

You can choose one of the following options:

- **Opt in to all current and future Instagram accounts** – Grants the CRM access to all your existing and future Instagram Professional accounts.

![Select Instagram Account](/img/facebook/facebook-step7a.png)

- **Opt in to current Instagram accounts only** – Allows you to manually select the Instagram account(s) you want to authorize.

![Select Instagram Account](/img/facebook/facebook-step7b.png)

If you choose **Opt in to current Instagram accounts only**:

1. Select the checkbox next to the Instagram account you want to connect.
2. Verify that the correct Instagram account is selected.
3. Click **Continue**.

:::note

Only the selected Instagram account(s) will be connected to the CRM and available for managing Instagram Direct Messages.

:::

---

## Step 8: Review and Grant Permissions

Meta displays a summary of the permissions that will be granted to the CRM.

Review the requested access, which may include:

- Managing your selected Business Account.
- Accessing your selected Instagram account profile and posts.
- Managing Facebook Messenger conversations.
- Managing Instagram Direct Messages.
- Managing Page settings, webhooks, and content.
- Viewing the Facebook Pages you manage.

After reviewing the requested permissions, click **Save** to complete the authorization.

![Review Permissions](/img/facebook/facebook-step8.png)

:::note

These permissions are required for the CRM to send and receive messages from Facebook Messenger and Instagram, synchronize conversations, and manage the connected channels.

:::

---

## Step 9: Complete the Connection

After the authorization is complete, Meta displays a confirmation message indicating that your Facebook account has been successfully connected to the CRM.

Click **Got it** to finish the authorization process.

![Connection Complete](/img/facebook/facebook-step9.png)

:::note

If you need to manage or revoke this connection in the future, you can do so through your Meta Business Integrations settings.

:::

---

## Step 10: Verify the Connection

After the Meta authorization process is completed, you are redirected back to the CRM.

A confirmation dialog is displayed indicating that the Facebook and Instagram account has been connected successfully.

Click **OK** to close the confirmation dialog.

![Connection Successful](/img/facebook/facebook-step10.png)

:::note

Connecting the Facebook Page automatically connects the linked Instagram Professional account.

:::
