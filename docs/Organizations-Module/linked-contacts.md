# Linked Contacts

The **Linked Contacts** tab provides a consolidated view of all Contacts associated with an Organization. It enables users to manage customer relationships at the account level by displaying every Contact linked to a specific Organization.

This centralized view helps sales teams understand the people associated with a business account and provides quick access to their contact information and activities.

---

## Overview

An Organization can have multiple Contacts representing different individuals such as decision-makers, managers, procurement teams, technical contacts, or other stakeholders.

The **Linked Contacts** tab serves as a central location for viewing and managing these relationships.

It displays all Contacts associated with the selected Organization, allowing users to maintain complete visibility into account-level relationships.


---

### Viewing Linked Contacts

To view linked Contacts:

1. Navigate to the **Organizations** module.
2. Open the required Organization record.
3. Select the **Linked Contacts** tab.

The system displays all Contacts associated with the Organization.

---

### Contact Summary

The Linked Contacts page provides a summary of the Contacts associated with the Organization.

The summary includes:

- **Total Linked Contacts** – Total number of Contacts associated with the Organization.
- **Primary Contact** – Primary point of contact for the Organization, if configured.
- **Active Contacts** – Contacts that are currently active and engaged.

These details provide users with a quick overview of the Organization's associated Contacts.

---

### Contact Information

Each linked Contact displays important information such as:

- Contact Name
- Job Title
- Email Address
- Contact Number
- Contact Owner
- Engagement Status

This information helps users quickly identify the appropriate person to contact within the Organization.


---

### Opening a Contact

Each Contact includes a **View Contact** option.

Selecting **View Contact** opens the corresponding **Contact Details** page, where users can:

- View complete Contact information.
- Review customer interactions.
- Manage tasks and meetings.
- Access linked Deals.
- Track communication history.

---

### Adding a New Contact

Users can create a new Contact directly from the **Linked Contacts** tab.

#### Steps

1. Open the required Organization.
2. Navigate to the **Linked Contacts** tab.
3. Click **Add Contact**.
4. Enter the Contact details.
5. Click **Save**.

The newly created Contact is automatically linked to the Organization.


---

### Relationship Management

Sales Astra CRM automatically maintains the relationship between Organizations and Contacts.

When a Contact is linked to an Organization:

- The Contact appears under the Organization's **Linked Contacts** tab.
- The Organization is displayed in the Contact record.
- Changes to the Contact are reflected across the CRM.
- The relationship remains intact unless it is manually updated.

This ensures consistent and accurate account management throughout the CRM.

---

### Benefits

The **Linked Contacts** tab helps users:

- View all Contacts associated with an Organization.
- Manage customer relationships from a single location.
- Quickly identify decision-makers and key stakeholders.
- Create new Contacts directly from the Organization.
- Access Contact records without leaving the Organization page.
- Maintain complete visibility into account-level relationships.
