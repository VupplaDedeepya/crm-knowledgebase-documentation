# Call Logs

The **Call Logs** section provides a centralized view of calls made or received through the configured calling providers.

Call Logs allow users to review call activity, access recordings and summaries, and convert eligible calls into Leads.

## Call Log Information

Call logs display information such as:

- **Caller** – Phone number associated with the call.
- **Provider** – Provider through which the call was made or received.
- **Time** – Date and time of the call.
- **Recording** – Availability of the call recording.
- **Summary / Notes** – Call summary or notes, when available.
- **Actions** – Actions available for the call.

---

## Call Recordings

When a recording is available for a call, the **Play** option is displayed in the Recording column.

Click **Play** to listen to the available recording.

If a recording is not currently available, the system displays an appropriate status such as **Not ready**.

---

## Call Summary and Notes

When a call summary is available, it is displayed in the **Summary / Notes** column.

Click **View more** to view the complete call summary or notes.

If the summary is not yet available, the system displays **Not ready**.

---

## Creating a Lead from a Call Log

A call that is not associated with an existing Lead can be converted into a Lead directly from the Call Logs page.

To create a Lead:

1. Locate the required call in **Call Logs**.
2. Click **Convert to Lead**.
3. Enter or review the required Lead information.
4. Create the Lead.

When the Lead is created from a call log, the **call summary is automatically added as a Note** to the newly created Lead.

This allows the sales representative to retain the important information captured during the call and refer to it later from the Lead record.

For example, if the call summary contains information about the customer's requirements, interests, or follow-up expectations, the summary is available as a Note on the created Lead.

:::note
The call summary is added to the Lead's Notes when the Lead is created from the Call Logs page.
:::

---

## Existing Lead Association

If a call is already associated with a Lead, the Actions column provides a link to open the associated Lead.

This allows users to quickly navigate from the call record to the relevant Lead.

---

## Filtering and Searching Call Logs

You can use the available filters to locate specific call records.

Call logs can be filtered by:

- **Date**
- **Provider**

You can also use the search field to find a specific call record.

---

## Managing Call Logs

The Call Logs page supports pagination when multiple call records are available.

From the Call Logs page, users can:

- View call records.
- Search for calls.
- Filter calls by date or provider.
- Play available recordings.
- View call summaries and notes.
- Convert eligible calls into Leads.
- Open the associated Lead.
- Retain the call summary as a Note when a Lead is created from the call.
